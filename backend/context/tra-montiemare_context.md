## Standard Property Rules & Logistics
- **Check-in Time**: Standard check-in is from **15:00 (3:00 PM)** onwards. Flexible check-in or early luggage drop-off can often be coordinated upon request.
- **Check-out Time**: Standard check-out is by **10:00 AM** (10:00).
- **Key Access / Check-in Process**: Self check-in via lockbox / keybox (access codes and digital guide provided prior to arrival).
- **Location & Accessibility**: Located in the historic pedestrian center of Scalea. It is a pedestrian-only area with a short scenic walk and traditional stone stairs (approx. 5-7 mins from parking, around 25 steps for Floor 1). Comfortable walking shoes are recommended.
- **Parking**: Public street parking (free and paid options) is available right outside the historic pedestrian area (approx. 5-7 minutes walk).
- **Transportation**: From Scalea-Santa Domenica Talao train station, taxi takes ~5 minutes (host can assist with taxi coordination), or a 15–20 minute walk (1.5 km).
- **Wi-Fi**: High-speed fiber Wi-Fi (100 Mbps) with dedicated workspaces suitable for remote work and digital nomads. The password is on the router/modem.
- **Air Conditioning & Heating**: A/C and heating are available (Piano 1 and Attico).
- **Kitchen & Amenities**: Fully equipped kitchen (Italian Moka coffee maker, stove, cookware, fridge), hair dryer, bed linens, towels, and washing machine.
