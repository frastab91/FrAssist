# Project Manifest: Rally NYC
Last Updated: 2026-08-30

## High-Level Overview
Rally NYC is a mobile-first web application designed for New York City tennis players to improve their court experience by providing community-reported crowd levels and wait times while facilitating connections between local players looking for hitting partners.

## Growth Objectives
- **Target Goal**: 1,000 active users (profiles)
- **Current Strategy**: Guerrilla on-court QR codes, tennis community bounties, and r/NYCtennis content loops.
