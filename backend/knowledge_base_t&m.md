SECTION 4: HOW TO GET THERE (TRANSPORT)
Q: What is the nearest airport to Scalea?
A: The most convenient airports to fly into are:

1) Lamezia Terme International Airport (SUF) - The closest airport, approximately 1 hour 15 minutes by train or car to Scalea. Direct trains run from the airport to Scalea.

2) Naples International Airport (NAP) - A popular option, approximately 2 hours by train to Scalea. There are direct trains from Naples to Scalea-S. Domenica Talao station.

3) Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO) - Also a great option, as there are high-speed trains from Rome that bring you directly to Scalea.


Q: How do I get from the airport to Scalea?
A: From Lamezia Terme (SUF):
- By train: Take a direct train from Lamezia Centrale to Scalea-S. Domenica Talao station (approximately 1 hour 15 minutes)
- By car: Rent a car at the airport and drive to Scalea (approximately 1 hour 15 minutes via the A2/E45 highway)

From Naples (NAP):
- By train: Take a train from Naples Centrale to Scalea-S. Domenica Talao station (approximately 2 hours)
- By car: Rent a car and drive (approximately 2 hours via the A3/A2 highway)

From Rome (FCO):
- By train: Take a high-speed train from Rome Termini to Scalea-S. Domenica Talao station


Q: How do I get from Scalea train station to the apartment?
A: From Scalea-S. Domenica Talao train station, you can:
- Take a taxi to the apartment (approximately EUR 15-25)
- We can arrange a pick-up service for you - just let us know your arrival time
- Some guests walk, but it is a bit of a distance uphill

We are happy to help coordinate transportation from the station. Please provide your arrival details when you book.


Q: Can I drive to Scalea?

SECTION 5: SCALEA & SURROUNDINGS
Q: What is Scalea like as a town?
A: Scalea is a lively town with a historic center, beautiful beaches, and authentic Italian character. It is a real Italian town, not just a tourist resort, with locals living there permanently. The town has a historic old town (the Centro Storico) with narrow cobblestone streets, cafes, and restaurants. It also has a beautiful beachfront with a promenade. Scalea is a great base for exploring the south of Italy, especially Calabria and nearby regions.


Q: How far is the apartment from the beach?
A: The beach is within walking distance from the apartment, approximately a 10-15 minute walk depending on your pace. The town has beautiful sandy beaches that are perfect for swimming, sunbathing, and relaxing.


Q: Is the apartment within walking distance to restaurants, markets, and cafes?
A: Yes! The apartment is at the edge of the historic center. Restaurants, markets, and cafes are all within 5-10 minutes walk. You can easily walk to shops, supermarkets, and local establishments.


Q: What is the weather like in different months?

- March-April: Mild and pleasant, perfect for exploring. Light layers recommended.
- May: Warm and sunny, ideal weather for outdoor activities.
- June: Warm to hot, beach weather. Sea temperatures are starting to warm up.

- November-April: Cooler months. Heating is available in the apartments.

October is described as a splendid time in Scalea, with swimming still possible at the beach.
