RallyNYC Update: We fixed the glitch

Hey fellow tennis addict,

Look, I'll be honest—I’m a tennis player building RallyNYC, not a full-time software engineer. Sometimes, that means things break. Specifically, that annoying bug that made messaging on mobile feel like trying to hit a backhand with a frying pan.

I apologize for the friction. I built this because I know how frustrating it is to show up to the courts and not have a partner, or worse, have no idea if the courts are packed.

The messaging issues are officially squashed. You can now coordinate your next hit without the app acting like it's never seen a smartphone before.

My goal hasn't changed: I just want to make sure you spend less time refreshing screens and more time actually rallying.

Go ahead, try it out. Send a message to a potential hitting partner—even if you're just inviting them to suffer through your double faults with you.
