# Tra-Montiemare: Canonical Scalea Local Guide & Recommendations

## 🍕 Pizzerias & Dining
- **La Rondinella Osteria Pop** (Via Vittorio Emanuele III, 7): Francesco's favorite restaurant in Scalea — outstanding local Calabrian dishes, pizza, and regional wine (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/V9H71gWeBZL6E1uE7?g_st=iwb`).
- **Pizzeria Mediterranea**: Cozy, beloved local favorite for traditional wood-fired pizza.
- **Cimalonga** (Centro Storico): Authentic Calabrian specialties in a historic setting near the apartment.
- **Antica Trattoria** (Centro Storico): Classic old-town trattoria for traditional recipes.
- **Fresco di Mare - Friggitoria di Mare** (Via Plinio Il Vecchio): Fresh seafood & fried fish with high quality at honest local prices (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/t59p3BmYSkPLxaHr6?g_st=iw`).
- **Ristorante Vigrì** (Via Napoleone, 11): Refined Scalea dining experience (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/wZXDt7BENDpSvWEi9?g_st=ic`).
- **Il Corsaro** & **Ristorante La Calura**: Beachfront seafood dining right on the water.
- **Ristorante Da Lucio (in Cirella)**: Highly scenic lunch destination in nearby Cirella (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/5junHjNeB48XAoZ39`).
- **Vecchio Frantoio**: Rustic countryside dining (car required) (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/RLLR9VgafSf6AxcGA`).

## ☕ Cafes, Breakfast & Drinks
- **Blend - Cocktail Bar** (Piazza Maggiore de Palma, 13): Best aperitivo & cocktail bar in the main piazza (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/Wsosq2ewNDTGRi4m8?g_st=ic`).
- **Bar de l'Unità**: Classic Italian morning coffee and casual aperitivo.
- **Bar Pasticceria Gelateria Daniela** (Via Garibaldi, 46): Fresh morning cornetti (croissants), espresso & artisanal gelato (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/PX3Kp2feAETvqtXC8?g_st=iwb`).
- **Mary Loù** (Via Tommaso Campanella, 58): Excellent bakery & pastries (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/GiyCYSA345gPFNVG8?g_st=iwb`).
- **Pasticceria Aronne (Marcellina)**: Renowned for traditional pastries with local Cedro (citron) (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/m1zSNeHMeKbUahwC8`).
- **Lido da Pietro** (Lungomare): Relaxed beach cafe for drinks by the sea (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/bGkiNsHraYk6mYbZ9?g_st=iwb`).

## 🛒 Supermarkets & Farmers Markets
- **Mercato Alimentari del Lunedì**: Monday morning open-air farmers market (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/u2hrMGMbA2ZgqnPJA`).
- **Alimentari Fornaro** (Via Fiume Lao, 27): Local grocer, 7 min walk (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/WiCQJubbgXGDRwUw9?g_st=ic`).
- **Interspar Supermarket** (Via Fiume Lao): Full-sized supermarket, 20 min walk (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/UdGGQd98Kg7UtauN9?g_st=ic`).

## 🏖️ Beaches, Day Trips & Activities
- **Spiaggia dell'Arcomagno (San Nicola Arcella)**: Iconic secluded sea cave & natural rock arch (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/KiaMTiDi8VTpYiue6?g_st=ic`).
- **Palazzo dei Principi**: Historic panoramic overlook where Francesco & Enerlida married (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/Sz7WJtR4Vn7w6mSD7`).
- **Pollino National Park & Orsomarso**: Nature trails, hiking, white-water river rafting.
- **Vera Italia (Chiara)**: Curated local tours & cultural experiences (`https://www.veraitalia.it/esperienze/`).
- **Car Rental (Noleggiami Italia Scalea)**: C.so Mediterraneo 139 (`https://share.google/JS7axpQKl8ILSZgpq`).
