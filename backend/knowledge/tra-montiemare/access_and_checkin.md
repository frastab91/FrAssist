# Tra-Montiemare: Access, Check-in & Logistics

## Guest Guide & Portal URL Construct
- **Canonical Guest Guide URL**: `https://www.tra-montiemare.it/guest?code=<ACCESS_CODE>`
- The `<ACCESS_CODE>` corresponds to the reservation / guest check-in code (e.g. `https://www.tra-montiemare.it/guest?code=IFBO6C`).

## Arrival & Drop-off Points
- **Primary / Usual Drop-off & Meeting Point**: **Piazza Maggiore De Palma** (often referred to as Piazza De Palma), located just at the edge of the historic pedestrian center.
- **Route to Apartment**: From Piazza De Palma, walk along the historic pedestrian path to **Vico Marittimo** / **Via Cimalonga 30**.
