## Standard Check-in & Check-out Times
- **Check-in**: From **15:00 (3:00 PM)** onwards (flexible self check-in via lockbox).
- **Check-out**: By **10:00 AM** (10:00).
- Early check-in or luggage drop-off can be arranged upon prior coordination.

## Location, Walking & Parking
- **Pedestrian Center**: Located in the historic pedestrian center of Scalea. Cobblestone paths and traditional stone stairs (approx. 5-7 minutes walk from parking, approx. 25 steps for Piano 1). Comfortable walking shoes are advised.
- **Parking**: Public street parking (free and paid options) is available outside the pedestrian boundary (5-7 minutes walk).
- **Train Station Access**: From Scalea-Santa Domenica Talao station: ~5 minute taxi (taxi coordination available) or 15–20 minute walk (1.5 km).

## Trash & Recycling Rules
- Separate waste into:
  - **Organico** (compostable bags)
  - **Plastica / Metalli** (yellow/transparent bags)
  - **Carta / Cartone** (paper bags)
  - **Vetro** (green glass bins)
  - **Secco Indifferenziato** (standard grey bags)
- **Bin Location**: Just a few steps away near the fountain at Piazza Maggiore de Palma (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/4Th9NvdMSMLZabADA?g_st=ic`).
- Guests must empty all waste bins before checking out.

## Guest Messaging Guidelines
- Provide ready-to-copy drafts directly in the chat with video links and access codes.
- Keep wording warm, natural, and generic ("non vediamo l'ora di darvi il benvenuto oggi!", "all'ingresso dell'appartamento").
- Warm closing: "Buon viaggio e a prestissimo! ☀️🏡".
