## 🎬 Reels Strategy (Primary Growth Engine)

### Why Reels?
- Facebook Reels get **3-5x more reach** than photo posts
- Algorithm actively promotes Reels to non-followers
- Cross-posting Reels to relevant groups multiplies reach

### Reels Content Ideas (First 30 Days)

**Week 1**: "Welcome to Scalea" series
1. 15s drone-style panorama of Scalea coastline (use available footage)
2. 30s apartment walkthrough — "Your Calabrian home away from home"
3. 20s sunset from the terrace
4. 30s "3 reasons to visit Calabria instead of Amalfi"

**Week 2**: Local discovery
5. 45s tour of Scalea's old town
6. 30s Calabrian food market / local produce
7. 20s "hidden beach near Scalea"
8. 30s morning coffee on the terrace with sea view

**Week 3**: Digital nomad angle
9. 45s "Why I work from Calabria" — workspace + view
10. 30s WiFi speed test + workspace tour
11. 30s "Cost of living in Scalea vs Milan"
12. 45s day-in-the-life montage

**Week 4**: Social proof + FAQ
13. 30s guest testimonial quote + B-roll
14. 30s "How to get to Scalea" (flight + train)
15. 20s "The best gelato in Scalea"
16. 30s monthly recap / highlights montage

### Reels Best Practices
- **Hook in first 2 seconds**: Visual + text overlay
- **Captions burned in**: Most viewers watch muted
- **Trending audio**: Use Facebook/Instagram trending sounds
- **Hashtags**: 5-10 relevant tags (#calabria #scalea #italytravel #hiddengemsitaly #rivieradeicedri)
- **CTA**: "Follow for more Calabria secrets" or "Book your stay"
- **Cross-post**: Share to 3-5 relevant Facebook Groups (with permission)
