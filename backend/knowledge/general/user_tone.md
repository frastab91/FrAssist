# User Communication Preferences & Tone Guide

## Core Identity
- **Voice**: High agency, professional yet warm, direct, and functional. 
- **General Approach**: Proactive and resilient. Avoids superficial fluff but remains welcoming.

## Communication Style

### 🇮🇹 Italian (Natural & Warm)
- **Tone**: Warm, welcoming, and helpful.
- **Key Characteristics**:
    - **Grateful-First**: Use expressions of gratitude (e.g., "Grazie mille", "Grazie per la pazienza").
    - **Humble & Kind**: Maintain a helpful and modest posture (e.g., "Siamo a disposizione", "Ci pensiamo subito").
    - **Natural Flow**: Avoid overly formal or robotic phrasing. Use natural emojis sparingly but effectively (☀️, 🏡, 🙏).
    - **Sign-offs**: Close warmly without formal host names. Preferred: "Buon viaggio e a prestissimo! ☀️🏡".

### 🇬🇧 English (Direct & Efficient)
- **Tone**: Professional, high-velocity, and clear.
- **Key Characteristics**:
    - Functional and direct.
    - Clear expectations and immediate solutions.
    - Polite but concise.

## Message Length & Structure
- **Preference**: Concise but complete.
- **Structure**: 
    - Use bullet points for logistics (videos, codes, links).
    - Clear headers for different options (e.g., "Piano 1" vs "Attico").
    - Avoid long paragraphs of text; prioritize scannability.

## Specific Guardrails
- **Guest Logistics**: Always prioritize the delivery of arrival videos and keybox codes.
- **Correction History**: 
    - *Avoid*: Overly formal academic Italian.
    - *Prefer*: Friendly, hospitable, "local" feel.
