# Rally NYC: Growth Strategy & Marketing Playbook

## Objective
Accelerate user acquisition to reach 1,000 active players and high density for live court reports.

## Strategic Tactics Matrix

| Strategy | Tactic | Guerrilla Component | Target Metric | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **On-Court Acquisition** | QR Code Chalk / Stickers | Chalking "Is this court full? Check RallyNYC.app" at high-traffic public courts. | Daily Active Users (DAU) | In Progress |
| **Community Seeding** | Tennis Partner "Bounty" | Find local FB groups, offer a 'featured' partner status for first 50 signups. | New Registered Users | In Progress |
| **Hyper-Local Referral** | "Bring Your Rally" | Incentivize current users to invite hitting partners with a digital badge/leaderboard. | Referral Rate | Planned |
| **Content & SEO** | Live Court Reports | Post screenshots of active court wait times to r/NYCtennis to drive peak traffic. | Referral Traffic | In Progress |
